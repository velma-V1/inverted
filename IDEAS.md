# Qwen Experimental Ideas

This is the index for experimental concepts related to Qwen testing, mentoring, learning, telemetry, and model modification.

These ideas are deliberately isolated from the preregistered Inverted benchmark. They do **not** change the benchmark design or evidence contract unless later promoted through a separate reviewed implementation.

## Status key

- **Seed** — captured, not yet fully designed
- **Designing** — architecture and controls are being specified
- **Ready** — protocol is complete enough to execute
- **Testing** — experiment is running
- **Proven** — evidence supports the hypothesis under stated conditions
- **Rejected** — evidence does not justify keeping it
