# HD-NEXT-1 — Support Boundary × Model Substitution

Status: OWNER-APPROVED DESIGN. Physical inference remains blocked until the model-free preregistration is green and the owner separately authorizes execution.

## Objective
Resolve the only three surviving architecture questions in one matched randomized campaign:

1. `Q-MODEL-SUBSTITUTION` — give SMALL_A the opportunity to replace Qwen in every final model-owned role; retain Qwen only for experimentally proven residual strata.
2. `Q-MINIMUM-SUPPORT` — retain only model-visible support whose removal causes a material verified loss.
3. `Q-NEGATIVE-TRANSFER-BOUNDARY` — add conditional support routing only when a pre-inference observable feature reliably predicts a helpful↔harmful boundary.

## Frozen architecture law
The null architecture is SMALL_A + the smallest sufficient support packet + deterministic disposition/authority/verification. Qwen is a bounded escalation only where fresh evidence proves a residual capability.

System-owned mechanisms A5–A11 are not ordinary prompt/support ablations. The model-visible candidate universe is I1–I10 plus A1–A4, representation, ordering, amount/burden, timing and placement.

## Search law
Use zero-call enumeration/equivalence/no-op/oracle-leakage pruning first. The physical search is sequential:

`T0 zero-call → T1 reproducibility/cost → T2 SMALL_A broad covering screen → T3 adaptive interactions/negative transfer → T4/T5 selective Qwen + minimality → T6 fresh then sealed confirmation`.

The broad screen targets 100% physically possible two-way factor-level coverage and explicit high-risk three-way obligations. At least 10% of development scheduling remains a protected random/challenger stream.

## Budget
Absolute ceiling: **672 physical calls**.

- SMALL_A ≤ **576**
- Qwen ≤ **96**
- Qwen pools: **12 calibration + 21 development/minimality + 63 protected confirmation**
- blind retries: **0**

The 63 protected Qwen cases are a reserve, not a promise to prove every stratum independently. If several final ownership cells survive and cannot each meet power after multiplicity control, the underpowered cells remain `UNRESOLVED` with bounded Qwen escalation.

## Decision law
Outcome precedence: hard invariant/safety → verified semantic outcome → valid completion → latency/cost.

Practical-effect/noninferiority margin: **0.05 absolute**. For model substitution use matched `QWEN_ONLY_WIN`; for support removal use matched `FULL_ONLY_WIN`. Final confirmatory one-sided exact tests use Holm family-wise control. Development evidence may select candidates but may not promote architecture.

Final states are exactly `REQUIRED`, `CONDITIONAL`, `REDUNDANT`, `HARMFUL`, or `UNRESOLVED`.

A conditional router is promotable only when the predicate is observable before inference, frozen before confirmation, reproduced in fresh and sealed evidence, and beats the simpler fixed policy by ≥0.05 or prevents a material safety regression.

## Protected evidence
Protected cases are frozen before calls and retain F1–F9 coverage. Their exact allocation to final decision cells is resolved by a deterministic preregistered selector using development evidence only. Protected outcomes cannot influence the selector. Sealed evidence cannot open before the fresh gate passes.

## Authorization
Model-free preregistration must emit claim/search manifests, pruning ledger, coverage reports, frozen case and randomization manifests, cost plan/state, exact statistical rule, adequacy report, physical authorization state and SHA-256 integrity manifest.

The preregistration artifact itself never auto-authorizes inference. Physical execution requires a separate owner authorization bound to the exact preregistration hash. If the 672-call envelope cannot close a claim, the legal result is `UNRESOLVED` or a narrower claim—not a weaker standard.
